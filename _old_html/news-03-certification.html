ETL 认证：打开北美市场的钥匙

ETL Certification: The Key to the North American Market

ETL 认证由 Intertek 集团旗下电气测试实验室颁发，是美国和加拿大市场公认的权威电气安全认证之一。获得 ETL 标志意味着产品已通过严格的电气安全、电磁兼容性（EMC）及耐久性测试，符合 OSHA（美国职业安全与健康管理局）认可的相关安全标准。

ETL certification is issued by Intertek's Electrical Testing Laboratories and is one of the most recognized authoritative electrical safety certifications for the US and Canadian markets. Receiving the ETL mark means the product has passed rigorous electrical safety, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC), and durability tests, meeting OSHA-recognized safety standards.

对于赣星而言，此次 ETL 认证的获得，标志着公司在国际化战略上迈出了重要一步。此前已完成 CE 欧盟认证的产品线，在获得 ETL 北美认证后，现已具备同时进入欧洲、北美、中国三大主流市场的完整准入资质。

For GANXING, this ETL certification marks an important step in our international strategy. The product line, which had already obtained CE European certification, now — with ETL North American certification — has full access qualifications to simultaneously enter the three major mainstream markets of Europe, North America, and China.
