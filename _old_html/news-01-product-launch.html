Breaking Performance Boundaries Through Technology

2026年1月1日，永康市赣星电动工具有限公司正式发布其最新一代旗舰抛光工具系列。此次发布涵盖汽车抛光机、金属表面处理机、石材水磨机、混凝土平整机、可调速角磨机及全系配件共六大系列。

On January 1, 2026, Yongkang Ganxing Power Tools Co., Ltd. officially launched its latest generation flagship polishing tool series. This launch covers six major series — car polishers, metal surface processors, stone wet grinders, concrete leveling machines, variable-speed angle grinders, and a full accessories range.

人体工学设计：让技术隐于操作

Ergonomic Design: Technology That Disappears Into Use

赣星始终坚持"以人为中心（Human-Centered Design）"的设计理念。2026款全系产品在握持角度、重心分布及振动控制（NVH）方面进行了全面调校。新款工具在连续工作状态下，操作者感受到的振动强度降低了约15%，显著减少长时间作业带来的疲劳感。

GANXING has always adhered to a Human-Centered Design philosophy. The 2026 full lineup has been comprehensively tuned in terms of grip angle, center-of-gravity distribution, and vibration control (NVH). The new tools deliver approximately 15% less perceived vibration during continuous operation, significantly reducing fatigue during extended use.
